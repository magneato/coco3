## What is covered
Neural Splines technology includes the methods used in [nsx6809/](nsx6809/) and its predecessor NSX86. Among them:
* the **Bresenham Gradient Descent** learning rule: training a network by diffusing gradient error into
  per-weight error terms and stepping integer weights when the error spills over;
* the fixed-point (8.8) training and inference methods and the model format built on it;
* the related Neural Splines methods for representing and training neural networks.

Other parts of this repository (wolf3d, fractals, cubes) may use some of the same techniques.
